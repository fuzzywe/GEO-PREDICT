What to Do During Natural Disasters?

Earthquake

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On under sturdy furniture.
  • Avoid windows, mirrors, and heavy objects.
  • Stay indoors until the shaking stops.
  • If outside, move to an open area away from buildings and power lines.

Flood

  • Move to higher ground immediately.
  • Avoid walking or driving through floodwaters.
  • Turn off electricity and gas before evacuating.
  • Listen to local warnings and evacuation orders.

Cyclone

  • Secure your home and avoid glass windows.
  • Stock up on food, water, and emergency supplies.
  • Evacuate if instructed by authorities.
  • Stay indoors in a safe room during the storm.
